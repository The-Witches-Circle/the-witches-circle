Our Game

A narrative-driven interrogation experience with a branching plot, set in a dystopian Orwell-inspired world. You are a regular person, Dough, who must undergo an interrogation procedure and possibly break free — yet the Interrogator knows their job well and will do anything to expose you.

  • Shape your protagonist through your answers — but some views might be violations of the law.
  • Deep, branching narrative gameplay leading to a variety of different outcomes.
  • Randomly picked persuasion mini-games to deceive or counteract the Interrogator.
  • Act differently to find the right approach, or face the consequences of your choices.

Who We Are

The Witches Circle is a tiny independent studio established by game industry enthusiasts. Right now, we are developing our debut game — an unconventional narrative experience featuring a branching dialogue system and a captivating, dark Orwell-inspired story that unfolds as you let it. We hope that our game will help to lift the veil from the injustice and cruelty that the interrogation experience can carry.
